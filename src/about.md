# About

This wiki is a plain-language guide to:

> You Y, Cui Y, Chen Y, He J, Wang D, Gasevic D, Wu X, Horvath S, Zhang M, Ma X. (2026). “Biological age in the meta omics era: Integrating physical activity, sleep, and diet to reshape paths of healthy ageing.” *iMetaOmics* 3: e70127. [doi:10.1002/imo2.70127](https://doi.org/10.1002/imo2.70127) · Open access, CC BY 4.0.

It was shared on X by Dr David Barzilai ([@agingdoc1](https://x.com/agingdoc1/status/2118096448562602139)) on 4 Nov 2026.

The paper is a short review (about 3,000 words, 20 references). Its authors include **Steve Horvath**, who created the first widely used epigenetic clock. Note his disclosed conflict of interest: the University of California owns patents on epigenetic biomarkers that name him as inventor, he founded and consults for the non-profit that licenses them, and he works at Altos Labs.

Because the paper is CC BY 4.0, the [full text](sources/fulltext.md) and Figure 1 are included here. Figure 2 (the research map) is described in words on the [Research Map](concepts/knowledge-map.md) page.
